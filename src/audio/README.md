# A real recording of the tune

Drop a recording in this folder and the page plays it instead of the
synthesised piano. Nothing else to change: the build picks up whatever is
here, and with the folder empty the page falls back to the tune it makes
itself.

    src/audio/tune.mp3      # or .ogg, or .m4a — the first one found wins

## It has to be a recording the page is allowed to serve

The **melody** is public domain — it has been since 2016, which is why this
site can play it at all. A **recording** of it is a separate copyright: it
belongs to whoever performed and produced it, and putting one on a public
page is publishing it.

So the file that goes here has to be one of:

- something you recorded yourself, or had recorded for you;
- a recording released under a licence that allows this — public domain,
  CC0, or a free-use library licence such as Pixabay's Content License
  (free to use, no attribution required, but the track may not be
  redistributed on its own);
- a recording you have bought or licensed for the purpose.

A track pulled off a video or a streaming service is none of those, and the
person who uploaded it is not the person who can give it away.

## What it costs

A recording is a download on a phone connection, which is the one thing the
synthesised tune was built to avoid: roughly 1 MB per minute at 128 kbps.
Under a minute is plenty — the page loops it.
